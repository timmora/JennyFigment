Quick Facts

📚

Books

Four titles: Lucky Penny (bilingual picture book, ages 4–9) and the Jenny Figment series — The Extraordinary Imagination of Jenny Figment, Andy's Space Friends, and The Riddle of the Fairytale Village (ages 6–11).

🎓

School visits

30-minute sessions for Lucky Penny, 45-minute sessions for the Jenny Figment books. Grades K–5, in Northern and Central Illinois.

🏪

Where to buy

Harvey's Tales, The Literary, Town House Books, and BookBaby online.
